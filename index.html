Pricing

Clear prices, no hidden fees. The final amount depends on size and level of detail.

A 50% GCash downpayment is required for every accepted commission. Prices marked "Price on quotation" depend on size, detail, and number of subjects. Fixed prices are standard and negotiable. Mention your budget in your request and Jake will discuss it with you.
